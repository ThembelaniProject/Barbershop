Clear information for a smooth, respectful booking experience at The Gents' Corner Barber Co.

1. Appointments

Appointments are confirmed after the booking form is successfully submitted. Please arrive approximately five minutes before your selected time so your service can begin promptly.

2. Opening hours

The shop is open Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 18:00 and is closed on Sundays. Appointment start times and service duration must fit within these hours.

3. Cancellations and changes

We ask customers to give reasonable notice when changing or cancelling an appointment. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may affect future booking availability.

4. Booking information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate name, email, phone, service, barber and appointment information. The booking form does not collect payment card details.

5. Personal information

Information submitted through the booking form is used to manage appointments and communicate about the requested service. We do not ask for unnecessary payment information through this website. Customers should avoid entering sensitive information in the notes field.

6. Calendar events

Google Calendar and downloadable calendar events are generated from the appointment selected by the customer. Calendar files downloaded to a device are under the customer's control and can be removed from their calendar at any time.

7. Website availability

We aim to keep booking information and website features available and accurate, but online availability can change. A booking confirmation should be retained as the customer's appointment record.

8. Contact

Questions about an appointment can be directed to 031 123 4567 or hello@thegentscorner.co.za. The shop is located at 12 Florida Road, Morningside, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.
